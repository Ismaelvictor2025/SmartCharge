# SmartCharge — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 30 October 2026

## About SmartCharge

SmartCharge is an educational iOS application developed as part of UTS 40005 Advanced iOS Applications. It helps users plan electric-vehicle journeys and assess charging options.

## Information and Local Storage

SmartCharge uses Apple's Core Data framework to store application information locally on the user's device, including vehicle profiles, journey information, preferences and favourite charging stations.

## Location and Maps

SmartCharge uses Apple MapKit for mapping and journey planning. Location access may be requested when required for location-based features. Map and routing requests may be processed by Apple under its applicable privacy terms.

## Notifications and Widgets

SmartCharge includes notification-related features and home-screen widget functionality. Notification permissions are controlled by the user through iOS settings. Widget information may be shared between the application and its widget extension using Apple's App Group storage.

## Third-Party Services

Apple platform services, including MapKit, may process information necessary to provide their functionality. Their handling of information is governed by Apple's applicable policies.

## Data Security and Retention

Locally stored application information is managed through iOS and the application's persistence mechanisms. Removing the application generally removes its local application data, subject to iOS backup and shared-storage behaviour.

## Educational Project

SmartCharge is an academic prototype and has not been publicly released through the Apple App Store. Its privacy disclosures must be reviewed and updated before any public release to reflect the actual application implementation and data flows.
